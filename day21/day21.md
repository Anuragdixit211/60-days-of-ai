# Day 21 - Digital Footprint Intelligence Dashboard

## Objective

Design and develop an advanced Digital Footprint Intelligence Dashboard to analyze digital exposure, privacy risks, and online presence using modern dashboard design principles.

## Project Overview

The dashboard provides insights into a user's digital footprint by visualizing privacy scores, risk levels, platform contributions, and AI-generated recommendations. It also includes a What-If Simulator to estimate privacy improvements based on user actions.

## Privacy Findings

### Key Findings

* Digital Risk Index: 78/100
* Privacy Score: 38/100
* High exposure across multiple online platforms
* Behavioral profiling risk identified
* Ad targeting probability is significantly high
* Google ecosystem contributes the largest share of digital footprint

### Recommendations

* Review third-party app permissions
* Disable unnecessary tracking options
* Remove unused social media accounts
* Enable multi-factor authentication
* Regularly audit connected services
