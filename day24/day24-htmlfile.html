Executive Summary

Local Business Chatbot is a WhatsApp-native AI assistant designed for Indian SMEs. The platform automates customer engagement, lead capture, appointment booking, FAQs, and follow-ups in Hindi and English. The primary market includes salons, clinics, gyms, coaching institutes, and service businesses that struggle to respond instantly to customer inquiries. The business follows a SaaS subscription model with monthly recurring revenue, low operational costs, and strong scalability.

Final Sustainability Verdict

If the startup achieves strong retention and referral-driven growth, it has the potential to become a sustainable venture.
