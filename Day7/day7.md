# Day 7 – Claude Model Selection & AI Workflow Strategy

## Objective

Learn how to choose the right Claude model and effort level based on different tasks and workflows.

---

## Models Explored

### Claude Haiku 4.5

**Best For:**

* Quick questions
* Fast answers
* Simple summaries
* Brainstorming ideas

**Key Learning:**
Haiku is the fastest Claude model and works best when speed matters more than deep reasoning.

---

### Claude Sonnet

**Best For:**

* Coding
* Learning
* Content creation
* Research
* Problem solving

**Key Learning:**
Sonnet provides the best balance between speed and quality, making it ideal for daily use.

---

### Claude Opus

**Best For:**

* Deep research
* Advanced coding
* Complex reasoning
* High-accuracy tasks

**Key Learning:**
Opus is the most powerful model and should be used when accuracy and detailed analysis are required.

---

## Recommended Effort Levels

### Low

* Quick answers
* Simple tasks

### Standard

* Daily learning
* Coding assistance
* Content creation

### High

* Important projects
* Detailed explanations

### Max

* Deep research
* Critical decision-making tasks
