Money tips for students

🎯
Follow a simple 50/30/20 split — needs, wants, savings — even on a small allowance.
🏷️
Always check for a student discount before buying software, transit passes, or food.
🔁
Review subscriptions every month — cancel anything unused before the renewal date.
⚠️
Avoid high-interest "buy now, pay later" apps — they're the most common student debt trap.
