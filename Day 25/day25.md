# 🦈 Day 25 of 60 Days Claude Challenge
# AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview

Today I built an **AI Shark Tank Simulator** that allows founders to pitch startup ideas to a panel of AI investors, answer challenging questions, receive startup evaluations, and get investment decisions based on business fundamentals.

The application simulates a realistic Shark Tank experience using AI-powered investors with different perspectives such as venture capital, product strategy, customer validation, and financial analysis.

## Key Learnings

### Product

- Simple startup ideas often perform better than complex ones.
- Customer pain points are more important than features.
- Clear positioning improves investor confidence.

### Business

- Investors prioritize scalability.
- Revenue clarity increases investment probability.
- Strong market size significantly impacts valuation.

### AI Insights

- Different AI investors evaluate businesses differently.
- Combining multiple viewpoints creates balanced startup feedback.
- AI can simulate realistic investor conversations effectively.
