## Key Learnings

1. Startup ideas must solve real customer problems.
2. Market validation is essential before product development.
3. TAM, SAM, and SOM help estimate business potential.
4. Competitor analysis reveals market gaps.
5. AI-powered SaaS products have strong scalability.
6. Customer interviews are crucial for validation.
7. Data-driven decisions improve startup success rates.
8. A professional validation report helps present startup ideas effectively to investors and stakeholders.
